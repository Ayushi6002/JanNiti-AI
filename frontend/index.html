How it works

Every application goes through a clear and accountable process.

01

Apply

Students submit academic and financial details, then upload the required supporting documents for their scholarship type.

02

Verify

Documents are checked for completeness and relevance, helping determine whether the application is ready for automated review.

03

Review

The AI recommends an outcome with reasons, while uncertain or sensitive cases are escalated for human evaluation.
